1. Scope of Services & No Legal Advice

NY MedCare provides medical examinations and administrative completion of USCIS Form I-693 by a designated civil surgeon. NY MedCare is not affiliated with U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) or the U.S. Department of Homeland Security. We do not provide legal advice. Any questions regarding immigration eligibility, visa status, or filing procedures should be directed to a qualified immigration attorney or accredited representative.

2. Medical Examination & Patient Responsibilities

Applicants are responsible for bringing valid, unexpired government identification, accurate vaccination records, and the correct edition of Form I-693 (edition 01/20/2025). The civil surgeon will conduct evaluations strictly in accordance with the CDC Technical Instructions for Civil Surgeons. Failure to disclose relevant medical history or comply with required laboratory tests (such as IGRA tuberculosis testing) will prevent the completion and sealing of your Form I-693.

3. Sealed Form I-693 Envelope Integrity

Your completed Form I-693 will be provided in a sealed envelope. You agree and understand that the sealed envelope must not be opened, broken, or tampered with prior to submission to USCIS. If the seal is compromised, USCIS will reject the form, and a re-issuance fee will apply for a new sealed envelope.

4. Appointment Booking & Cancellation

Online appointments are scheduled through our third-party scheduling software (GoHighLevel). We request at least 24 hours advance notice if you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment.

5. Fees & Payment

Immigration medical examinations are self-pay services and are generally not billable to health insurance plans. Payment in full is required at or before the time of service. Third-party laboratories and retail pharmacies may bill your insurance separately for lab analyses or vaccine doses where eligible.
