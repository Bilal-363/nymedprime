Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Reviewed: March 2026

At NY MedCare, led by Dr. Huma Irshad, MD, we understand the critical importance of privacy and confidentiality when undergoing an immigration medical examination for U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal and health information.

1. Protected Health Information (HIPAA Compliance)

As a medical practice operating in the State of New York, NY MedCare adheres strictly to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA). All medical records, laboratory test results, vaccination histories, and examination findings obtained during your Form I-693 evaluation are treated as Protected Health Information (PHI) and maintained in secure, encrypted medical record systems.

2. Information We Collect

To perform your USCIS immigration medical examination, we collect:

  • Identification Details: Full legal name, date of birth, address, phone number, email, government-issued photo identification (passport, driver's license), and Alien Registration Number (A-Number) or USCIS receipt notice (if applicable).
  • Clinical & Vaccination Data: Past medical history, immunization records, prior tuberculosis screenings or treatments, current medications, and diagnostic laboratory findings (IGRA blood test, syphilis, gonorrhea).
  • Booking & Communication Information: Details provided when reserving your appointment online via our integrated GoHighLevel calendar or by telephone.

3. How Your Information is Used

Your information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Conducting the official immigration medical examination mandated by USCIS and the CDC.
  • Completing and signing your official Form I-693 (Report of Immigration Medical Examination and Vaccination Record).
  • Ordering required laboratory tests and issuing immunization prescriptions.
  • Contacting you regarding laboratory results and appointment status.

4. Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your completed Form I-693 is placed into a tamper-evident sealed envelope and provided directly to you for submission to USCIS. We also provide you with an identical unsealed copy for your personal records. NY MedCare does not directly transmit your records to immigration authorities unless required by lawful subpoena or public health reporting statutes (such as mandatory reporting of active communicable diseases under CDC/New York State Department of Health guidelines).
